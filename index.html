Woven in Bengal, worn with pride in the USA

Desi Romoni (দেশি রমণী — "woman of the homeland") brings the clothing Bengali women grow up loving to doorsteps across America. The feather-light Jamdani of Dhaka's looms, the classic white-and-red laal-paar saree worn at Durga Puja, soft muslin that breathes through summer — pieces that carry home with them.

Whether it's Eid morning, a wedding in Queens, or Boishakh at the park, dress like it matters — because to us, it does.

Real handloom, real weavers

Jamdani and muslin sourced from Bengal's weaving traditions — no printed imitations passed off as handwoven.

Every photo is the real piece

What you see in this album is the exact saree or set — folded, laid out, dupatta and pants included in the photos.

Blouse piece included

Every saree comes with its matching blouse piece, and kameez sets arrive as complete three-pieces with dupatta.
